# Your first 10 minutes

You've installed Claude Code and the `ai-beginner-augmentor` pack ([getting started](getting-started.md)). Here's a guided first session. Pick the section that fits you, or use "Everyone" if none do.

Three habits for every session:

1. **Say who it's for and what you want.** ("For my manager, a 5-line summary.")
2. **Check anything that matters.** Names, numbers, dates, links. Run `/ai-beginner-augmentor:check-this` on any answer.
3. **Don't paste secrets.** No passwords, ID numbers, bank details, or other people's private info.

## Everyone (any background)

| Minute | Do this | What you'll get |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 | Type `/ai-beginner-augmentor:start` | A friendly hello and two questions about you |
| 2–5 | Answer them, then try your small task together | A first draft or plan, made with you |
| 5–7 | Type `/ai-beginner-augmentor:check-this` and paste that answer | A list of what to double-check and where |
| 7–9 | Type `/ai-beginner-augmentor:practice` and pick an exercise | A 3-minute exercise and kind feedback |
| 9–10 | Type `/ai-beginner-augmentor:which-pack` | One pack suggestion that fits you, with the install command |

## Students

1. `/ai-beginner-augmentor:ask-better explain photosynthesis` and follow the coaching to add your level and what you're stuck on.
2. Ask your improved question.
3. `/ai-beginner-augmentor:check-this` on the answer, and compare with your textbook.
4. Check your course's AI rules. Next pack: `student-augmentor` (it coaches; it won't write graded work for you).

## Office and knowledge workers

1. Paste an email you need to reply to, **with names and numbers removed** ("my manager", `[amount]`).
2. Ask: "Draft a polite reply, under 100 words. Ask me questions first if anything is unclear."
3. Edit the draft yourself. You send it; Claude never does.
4. Check your workplace's AI policy. Next pack: `office-augmentor`.

## Parents and caregivers

1. Type: "Help me turn this week's schedule into a simple plan" and describe it in your own words (first names or roles only).
2. Try `/ai-beginner-augmentor:practice steps` for a quick exercise.
3. For anything medical or about benefits, use it to prepare questions for the professional, not as advice. Next pack: `parent-caregiver-augmentor`.

## Trades and hands-on work

1. Type: "Help me turn this job into a list of steps" and describe a job without the customer's name or address.
2. Ask it to point out anything you should confirm, like code rules or prices. It should mark those `[CHECK: …]` rather than guessing.
3. Next pack: `trades-augmentor` (quotes use only your rates; code questions go to your adopted code and inspector).

## Job seekers

1. Paste one bullet from your resume and ask `/ai-beginner-augmentor:ask-better make this resume bullet stronger`.
2. Make sure every fact in the result is true. Remove anything you didn't actually do.
3. Next pack: `job-seeker-augmentor`.

## Healthcare workers

1. **Never paste patient information.** Use a made-up example.
2. Try `/ai-beginner-augmentor:practice privacy` to practice de-identifying.
3. Next pack: `healthcare-worker-augmentor` (not medical advice; your facility's policy and references come first).

## Deciding what to buy, pick, or plan

1. Install the pack: `/plugin install decision-coach-augmentor@augmentor-packs`.
2. Type `/decision-coach-augmentor:grill-me` and what you're deciding ("a new phone plan"). Answer one question at a time; say "enough, go" when you're ready.
3. Check the summary it writes, then let it search the web (Claude Code may ask your permission). Every fact comes with a link and a date.
4. You decide. It never buys or signs you up. Don't share account numbers or passwords.

## Teachers, creators, founders, designers, engineers

Run `/ai-beginner-augmentor:which-pack` and tell it what you do. It will match you to `educator-augmentor`, `creator-augmentor`, `solo-founder-augmentor`, `designer-augmentor`, `engineer-augmentor`, or another pack, with its [level](../index.md#levels).

## When you're done

- Type `/exit` (or close the window) to end the session.
- Tomorrow, run `/ai-beginner-augmentor:start` again and ask for Day 2.
