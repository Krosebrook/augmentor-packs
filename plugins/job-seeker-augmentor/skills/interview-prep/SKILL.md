# Interview Prep

1. Inputs: posting, interview stage and format, interviewer roles if known, the user's resume, and company facts the user has gathered. Don't invent company facts — give a **research checklist** instead (product, customers, recent news from sources the user reads, mission/values, team) with `[CONFIRM]` on anything the user should verify.
2. **Likely questions** grouped: motivation, role-specific/technical (from posting requirements), behavioral (map to the star-story-bank), and curveballs (gaps, job hopping, salary expectations).
3. **Pitch:** a 60–90 second "tell me about yourself" (present → past → why this role), built from the user's real history.
4. **Tricky answers:** weaknesses (real, with what they're doing about it), employment gaps (brief, honest, forward-looking), reasons for leaving (no badmouthing).
5. **Questions to ask:** 5–8 thoughtful ones by interviewer type.
6. Logistics checklist: time zone, link/address, materials, follow-up thank-you plan.

Offer a mock interview next.
