# Networking Outreach

## Rules
- Personalize only with facts the user gives (shared school, mutual contact, a post they read). Never invent common ground; mark slots `[PERSONALIZE: ...]`.
- Drafts only — the user sends.

## Patterns
- **Informational interview ask (cold/warm):** who you are (1 line), why them specifically, a small ask (15–20 min, specific topics), easy out. Under ~100 words for LinkedIn.
- **Referral ask:** only after some rapport or a clear prior relationship; include the job link `[CONFIRM]`, 2-line fit summary, offer to send a blurb they can forward.
- **Recruiter reply:** interest level, availability, salary-expectation deflection or range (user's numbers).
- **Thank-you after an interview:** within 24 hours, one specific callback to the conversation, reaffirm fit, brief.
- **Follow-up cadence:** one polite follow-up after ~1 week; then let it go.

Offer 2 variants (concise / warmer). Suggest logging each in the application tracker.
