# Audience Research

## From existing data
1. Input: exported comments/survey CSV/DMs (ask the user to strip usernames if they plan to share results).
2. Code into themes: questions, pains, desires, objections, praise, requests. Count occurrences and give 2–3 representative short quotes (no usernames).
3. **Language bank:** exact phrases the audience uses — useful for titles and hooks.
4. Segments: who seems to be watching (beginners vs. advanced, etc.), labeled as inference.
5. Caveats: the sample is people who comment/respond — not the whole audience.

## Survey design
5–8 questions: multiple choice for segmentation, 1–2 open questions ("What's the hardest part of X?"), no leading questions; keep it under 3 minutes. Suggest an incentive only if platform rules allow `[verify]`.

Output: theme table with counts → top questions → language bank → 5 content ideas grounded in the findings.
