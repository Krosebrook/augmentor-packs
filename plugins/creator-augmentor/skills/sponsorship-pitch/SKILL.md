# Sponsorship Pitch & Media Kit

## Rules
- Only the user's real numbers (subscribers, average views over a stated period, open rate, demographics from platform analytics). Missing → `[DATA NEEDED]`.
- No invented testimonials or past partners → `[TESTIMONIAL NEEDED]`.
- Rates come from the user; if they ask what to charge, explain how to reason from their own views/CPM data and costs and leave `[DATA NEEDED: your rate]` — no invented industry rates.

## Pitch email
Subject → why this brand (specific fit from facts the user gives `[CONFIRM]`) → audience fit in 1–2 data points → concrete integration idea (format, placement, deliverables) → proof (real past results if any) → clear ask (call, rate card). Under ~150 words. The user sends.

## Media kit outline
Bio, audience snapshot (with period and source), formats offered, deliverables and rates `[DATA NEEDED]`, past partners (real only), contact.

## Negotiation & terms checklist
Deliverables, usage rights/whitelisting duration, exclusivity, approval rounds, payment terms, kill fee. **Disclosure:** sponsored content must be disclosed per platform rules and local advertising law `[verify: platform & local ad-disclosure rules]`. Not legal advice on contracts.
