# Voice Consistency

## Build a voice guide
1. Ask for 3–6 samples the user considers most "them" (scripts, transcripts, posts).
2. Extract: tone (e.g. warm/irreverent/precise), sentence length and rhythm, vocabulary they use and avoid, humor style, how they open and close, recurring phrases/sign-offs, how they handle facts and sources, perspective (I/we/you), taboo topics.
3. Write `voice-guide.md` (with permission): 3–5 voice traits each with "this, not that" examples drawn from their samples; do/don't lists; formatting conventions.

## Check a draft
Compare against the guide: list sentences that sound off-voice with suggested rewrites, generic AI-sounding phrases to cut (e.g. "in today's fast-paced world", "let's dive in" if not theirs), and consistency issues.

The user's voice evolves — offer to update the guide quarterly. Never clone another creator's identity or catchphrases.
