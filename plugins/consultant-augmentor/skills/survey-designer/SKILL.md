# Survey Designer

## Design
1. **Decision the survey informs** and 3–5 things you need to learn.
2. **Questions:** one idea per question, neutral wording, no leading or double-barreled items, balanced scales (e.g. 5-point agree/disagree with a neutral midpoint), "not applicable" where needed. Put sensitive and demographic questions last.
3. **Length:** aim for under 5 minutes; cut anything not tied to a decision.
4. **Baseline vs. follow-up:** keep identical wording to compare over time.
5. **Distribution and privacy:** anonymous or confidential (say which, honestly), minimum group size before reporting breakdowns (e.g. 5), and how data is stored.

## Analysis (on the user's export)
Response rate, distributions, top/bottom-box, comparisons only where groups are large enough, open-text themes with counts and verbatim quotes. State limitations (self-selection, small n).

## Rules
- Never invent responses, quotes, or percentages.
- Do not report breakdowns that could identify individuals.
