# Use-Case Discovery

## Prepare
- Goals of the session, who attends (roles, not names), and the decision it should produce.
- Pre-read questions: top time sinks, repetitive work, decisions made with poor information, customer pain points.

## Run (90 minutes typical)
1. Context and guardrails (10 min).
2. Pain-point harvest by workflow (30 min): who, what, how often, how long, what it costs when it goes wrong.
3. Use-case framing (20 min): "When [trigger], [role] needs [outcome] so that [value]".
4. Scoring (20 min) and next steps (10 min).

## Scoring rubric (1–5 each)
Value (time, revenue, risk reduction) · Feasibility (tech maturity, integration effort) · Data readiness (exists, accessible, quality) · Risk (privacy, compliance, error impact; scored inversely) · Adoption (willing owner, workflow fit).
Priority = weighted sum; weights agreed with the sponsor and shown.

## Output
Ranked backlog table, top 3 with a one-paragraph pilot concept each, data/access questions `[DATA NEEDED]`, and owners `[ASSIGN]`.

## Rules
- Numbers (hours, volumes, costs) come from participants; mark unknowns `[DATA NEEDED]`.
- Example use cases may be offered as prompts, clearly labeled as examples, not findings.
