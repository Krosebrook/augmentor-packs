# Workshop Designer

## Inputs
Audience (roles, experience level, attitude toward the topic), size, duration, in-person or remote, the decision or capability the session must produce.

## Design
1. **Objectives:** 2–3, phrased as what participants can do or decide by the end.
2. **Agenda:** timed blocks; alternate input and activity; no lecture longer than 15 minutes; include breaks over 90 minutes.
3. **Exercises:** for each, purpose, instructions as read aloud, timing, group size, materials, and the output artifact.
4. **Hands-on beats slides:** for AI sessions, use participants' own (de-identified) tasks for live practice.
5. **Facilitation notes:** openers, handling skeptics (ask for their best objection and test it), quieting dominant voices (silent writing first), parking lot.
6. **Close:** decisions, owners, dates, and a 3-question feedback form.

## Rules
- Remind participants not to paste confidential or personal data into tools without approval.
- Do not promise outcomes; follow-up actions are owned by named roles `[ASSIGN]`.
