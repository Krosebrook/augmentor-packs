# Doctor Visit Prep

## Boundaries
Not medical, legal, financial, or benefits advice. Doctors, pharmacists, social workers, elder-law attorneys, benefits counselors, and the school are the authorities; this skill helps you prepare and organize. Eligibility rules, coverage, and program names vary by country/state — mark them `[verify: official source / professional]`. Emergencies: call your local emergency number.

## Build
1. From the user's notes: main concern in one sentence; **symptom timeline** (when it started, frequency, triggers, what helps, what they've tried); relevant history; current medications/supplements with doses **as the user lists them** `[CONFIRM against bottles]`; allergies.
2. **Top 3 questions** first (time is short), then more: What could be causing this? What tests and why? What should we watch for, and when should we call or go to urgent care? What are the options and trade-offs? When is follow-up?
3. One-page summary for the clinician.
4. After the visit: a template to record what was said, new instructions, and follow-ups (feeds care-log).
5. Portal messages: concise, one issue, clear question. Urgent symptoms → call the clinic/emergency services, not a portal message.

Never suggest a diagnosis, medication, or dose.
