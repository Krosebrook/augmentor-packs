# School Communication

## Principles
Calm, specific, collaborative: what you observed (facts, dates the user gives), the impact, your question or request, and an offer to partner. One topic per email; short.

## Common drafts
- **Concern/grades:** observation → question → request for a meeting or suggestions.
- **Absence/late work:** brief reason (share only what's needed), request for missed work.
- **Bullying/safety:** factual account with dates and what the child reported, request for the school's process and a follow-up date. Urgent safety → call the school and, if needed, authorities.
- **Accommodations (504/IEP or local equivalent):** request an evaluation or meeting in writing, keep a copy. Rights and timelines vary by jurisdiction `[verify: official source / special-education advocate]`.
- **Conference prep:** 3 priorities, questions to ask, what you've seen at home, desired next steps.
- **Thank-you:** specific and brief.

Give a warm and a more formal version on request. The user sends. Not legal advice.
