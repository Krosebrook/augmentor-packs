# Family Logistics

1. Gather (paste or list): each person's fixed commitments, activities, work hours, transport options, recurring tasks, and known one-offs this week.
2. Build a **week grid** (day × time) with owner per item; highlight **conflicts** (two places at once, no driver) and propose 2 options each — the user decides.
3. Rides/carpool table: who, where, when, backup.
4. Chores: age-appropriate assignment suggestions with simple rotation; the family adjusts.
5. Checklists on request: back-to-school, sports bag, travel packing, morning launch-pad routine.
6. Output: a shareable Markdown/CSV the user can paste into their calendar or family chat (they send it).
7. Keep it light: flag overloaded days and suggest what could be dropped, not just squeezed in.
