# Parent & Guardian Communication

Drafts only — the teacher reviews and sends through school-approved channels.

## Rules
- **Facts, not labels:** describe observable behavior and specific work ("hasn't submitted the last 3 homework assignments"), never diagnoses or character judgments ("lazy", "probably ADHD").
- **Partnership framing:** shared goal (the student's success), what school is doing, what would help at home, how to reach you.
- **Plain language:** short sentences, no education jargon or acronyms without explanation; aim for a reading level most families can access.
- **Privacy:** don't mention other students; keep sensitive matters (discipline details, special education, health) to appropriate channels — suggest a call or meeting for anything serious.
- **Policy check:** follow school communication policies and escalation paths (counselor, admin) for safety concerns or mandated-reporting issues — that's outside a parent email.
- Use `[CHECK: …]` for any date, grade, or detail the teacher must confirm.

## Templates
- **Positive note:** specific thing the student did + why it matters + thanks. 3–4 sentences. (Send these early — they make hard conversations easier later.)
- **Concern:** warm opener → specific observation with dates/examples → impact on learning → what you're doing → a specific request or invitation to talk → contact info and availability.
- **Responding to an upset parent:** acknowledge the concern, state facts calmly, explain the reason/policy, offer a conversation, avoid defensiveness or arguing point-by-point in email. Suggest moving complex disputes to a call or meeting.
- **Newsletter/update:** what we're learning, upcoming dates, how to help at home, one celebration.

## Translation
If the teacher needs other languages, produce a clean, idiom-free English version suitable for translation and recommend the school's official translation/interpretation services for important or sensitive messages. If asked to translate directly, label it as machine translation needing review by a fluent speaker.
