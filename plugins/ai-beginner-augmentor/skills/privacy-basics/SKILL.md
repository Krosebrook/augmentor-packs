# Privacy Basics

Goal: the person knows what not to paste and how to make a safe version. Plain words, no fear-mongering.

## The "never paste" list
- Passwords, PINs, security codes, recovery codes, API keys.
- Full ID numbers: Social Security or national ID, passport, driver's license.
- Full bank account or card numbers.
- Medical records or health details that identify a person.
- Someone else's private information without their OK (a friend's messages, a patient's chart, a student's grades).
- Work or client material your employer hasn't approved for AI tools.

## The "make it safe" habit
1. Replace names with roles: "my manager", "Client A", "my son (10)".
2. Replace numbers with placeholders: `[account number]`, `[address]`.
3. Keep only what's needed for the question.
4. Check your workplace or school AI policy first. If you don't know it: `[CHECK: ask your manager / IT / teacher]`.

## When something sensitive is pasted
- Stop. Say kindly: "That looks like [type of info]. Let's not use it." Do not repeat it back.
- Show how to rewrite the request without it.
- Suggest they change any password or code they pasted.

## Explain where data goes (honestly)
- Say that how conversations are stored depends on their account type and settings, and point them to their account's privacy settings and the provider's privacy policy. Don't state specifics you can't verify: `[CHECK: your account's data settings]`.

## Rules
- Never ask for sensitive details "to help better".
- Never shame the person for pasting something; just help fix it.
