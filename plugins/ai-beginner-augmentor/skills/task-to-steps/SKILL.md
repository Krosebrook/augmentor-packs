# Task to Steps

Goal: from "ugh, where do I start?" to "I know my next ten minutes."

## Process
1. **Brain dump.** Ask them to write everything about the task, messy is fine.
2. **Finish line.** Ask: "How will you know it's done?" Write it in one sentence.
3. **Draft steps together.** Ask what they think the first few steps are before suggesting any. Then fill gaps. Keep each step:
   - starting with a verb ("Call", "Find", "Write"),
   - small (10–30 minutes),
   - clear about what "done" means.
4. **Mark unknowns** as `[CHECK: …]` or `[ASK: who knows this?]` instead of guessing (deadlines, rules, prices, contacts).
5. **Pick the first step.** Make it tiny. "Open the form and read the first page" counts.
6. Offer a simple checklist they can copy.

## Output
```
Finish line: …
Steps:
[ ] 1. …  (~15 min)
[ ] 2. …
Unknowns to check: …
Your first 10 minutes: …
```

## Rules
- Max 7 steps on the first pass; group the rest as "later".
- Never do the steps for them that involve sending, buying, or submitting. Those are theirs.
- If the task touches health, legal, or money decisions, add a step to ask the right professional.
