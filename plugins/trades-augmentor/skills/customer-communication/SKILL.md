# Customer Communication

## Principles
- Short and specific: what's happening, what it means for them, what's next, who to contact.
- Match the channel (text ≈ 1–3 sentences; email can carry detail).
- No commitments the user didn't make: dates, prices, warranty, or "we'll cover it" → `[CONFIRM]`.
- Explain technical issues in plain language with one analogy at most; no jargon dumps.

## Templates (adapt)
- **On my way / running late:** ETA, apology once, option to reschedule.
- **Estimate follow-up:** reference the quote, one benefit, an easy next step, no pressure.
- **Delay:** cause (briefly), new expected date `[CONFIRM]`, what you're doing about it, options.
- **Change order:** what was found, why it matters (safety/code/function), options with prices from the user, and "nothing changes until you approve in writing".
- **Payment reminder:** friendly → firm sequence, invoice number, amount, due date, how to pay. Not legal advice on liens/collections.
- **Review request:** thank-you first, one link `[CONFIRM: review link]`, no incentives that violate platform rules.
- **Complaint:** acknowledge, facts, proposed fix, offer a call.

Give two tones (friendly / formal) when useful. The user sends.
