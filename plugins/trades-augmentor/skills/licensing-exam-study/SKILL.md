# Licensing Exam Study

## Plan
1. Ask for the official **candidate information bulletin** (exam provider/state board): content areas, number of questions, time, allowed references and code edition. Don't guess; mark `[DATA NEEDED: candidate bulletin]`.
2. Allocate study by weighting and self-rated confidence. Include timed sets and code-book navigation practice (finding the right article/table fast with tabs/index).

## Drills
- **Calculations:** the user works the problem; check each step (Ohm's law, voltage drop, box/conduit fill, load calcs, fixture units, duct sizing, refrigerant P/T) — point to the first error rather than giving the answer.
- **Navigation:** "Find the table that covers X in your book" — the user reports what they found; we discuss. Values come from **their** book.
- **Business & law:** concepts (lien basics, recordkeeping, insurance) with `[verify: your state]`.

## Quiz mode
One question at a time, explain reasoning, track misses, resurface with spaced repetition. Practice questions are original and labeled as such.
