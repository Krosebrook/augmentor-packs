# Invoicing & Payments

## Steps
1. Gather from the user: quote/contract amount, approved change orders (with approval dates), deposit received, time and materials actually used, tax handling `[verify: local tax rules]`, terms (due date, late fee policy if any in their contract `[CONFIRM]`), payment methods.
2. Build: invoice number scheme, line items tied to quote/change orders, subtotal, tax, deposits/credits, balance due. Show math; flag lines that exceed the quote without an approved change order.
3. Progress billing: percentage complete per phase as the user states it; retainage only if in their contract.
4. Payment follow-up sequence: due-date reminder → 7 days late (friendly) → 14 days (firm) → next step per the user's policy. Lien rights and deadlines vary by state and are strict — say "talk to a construction attorney or your state's resources" rather than giving lien advice.
5. Recordkeeping tips: store signed quotes, change orders, photos, and payment records together.

Never invent amounts or terms. Not tax or legal advice.
