# AI-Look Detector

Generic output is a symptom of missing decisions. This skill names the defaults and asks what the deliberate choice would be.

## Tells to check
- **Layout:** centered hero, three-feature card row, testimonial carousel, CTA band, all at equal weight; no clear focal point.
- **Color:** purple-to-blue gradients, glassmorphism everywhere, low-contrast gray-on-gray, accent used on everything.
- **Type:** one family at three sizes; no scale; all-caps eyebrow labels on every section.
- **Icons:** mixed libraries or styles, emoji as icons, icons that decorate rather than inform, inconsistent stroke width or grid.
- **Copy:** "Unlock", "Seamless", "Elevate", "Supercharge", vague benefits with no specifics, invented stats or logos.
- **Imagery:** stock 3D blobs, generic people at laptops, inconsistent illustration styles.

## Output
For each tell found: `where → the default → why it reads generic → a deliberate alternative tied to the brief`. End with the 3 changes that would add the most character.

## Rules
- Taste is the user's. Offer options, not a mandate.
- Flag any statistic, logo, or testimonial that looks invented: `[CHECK: source]`.
