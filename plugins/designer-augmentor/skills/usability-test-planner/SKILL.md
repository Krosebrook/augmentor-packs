# Usability Test Planner

## Plan
1. **Decision this test informs** and the 2–4 research questions.
2. **Participants:** criteria and screener questions; 5 per distinct user group is usually enough to find major issues in a qualitative test.
3. **Tasks:** realistic scenarios with a goal, not instructions ("You want to change your delivery date" not "Click Settings"). Define success criteria per task.
4. **Script:** intro and consent, warm-up, tasks with neutral probes ("What are you looking for?"), post-task and closing questions. Remind the moderator not to lead.
5. **Metrics:** task success, time on task, errors, and a post-test questionnaire such as SUS or SEQ if useful.
6. **Note grid:** participant x task with observations, quotes, and severity.

## Synthesis (from the user's notes)
Cluster observations into issues, count how many participants hit each, rate severity, and separate **observed** from **interpreted**. Quotes must be verbatim from the notes; if absent write `[QUOTE NEEDED]`.

## Rules
- Never invent participants, quotes, or results.
- Small qualitative samples do not support percentages or statistical claims; say so.
