# Outreach Drafts

## Rules
- **Drafts only.** The founder reviews, personalizes, and sends.
- **Real personalization only.** Use facts the founder gives you about the recipient. Otherwise leave `[PERSONALIZE: something specific you noticed about them]`. Never invent a mutual connection, a post they wrote, or a compliment.
- **Respect the law and the recipient:** follow applicable rules for commercial messages (e.g., CAN-SPAM in the US; GDPR/PECR in the UK/EU; CASL in Canada) — include honest sender identity and a way to opt out where required. This isn't legal advice; the founder should check what applies to them.
- **No deception:** no fake "Re:" subjects, fake urgency, or pretending to be a customer.

## Message formula (≤120 words for cold)
1. **Why them** (specific, 1 line).
2. **The relevant problem/observation** in their terms (1–2 lines).
3. **Credibility or proof** — one real thing (1 line).
4. **One low-friction ask** — a question or a 15-min call, or "worth a look?" — not a demo + call + trial.
5. Plain signature.
Subject lines: short, specific, lowercase-casual is fine; no clickbait.

## Sequences
Default: initial + 2 follow-ups spaced several business days apart, each adding new value (a relevant resource, a short insight, a different angle), and a polite close-the-loop final message. Stop on any reply or "no."

## Variants
For each outreach goal, give 2 versions (e.g., problem-led vs. curiosity-led) so the founder can test, and suggest tracking reply rate per version over at least a few dozen sends before concluding anything.

## Tracking template
`recipient | segment | version | sent date | follow-up dates | reply? | outcome | notes`.
