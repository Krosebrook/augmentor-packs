# Financial Basics & Runway

## Definitions (use these precisely)
- **Gross burn:** total cash out per month.
- **Net burn:** cash out − cash in per month.
- **Runway (months):** current cash ÷ average net burn (use a recent 3-month average if the founder has it).
- **Break-even:** monthly revenue (after variable costs) ≥ monthly fixed costs.
- **Gross margin:** (revenue − direct cost to deliver) ÷ revenue.
- **Contribution per customer:** price − variable cost per customer.

## Rules
- **Only the founder's numbers.** Ask for: cash in bank, monthly fixed costs (itemized), variable costs per customer/sale, current revenue and its trend, founder salary/draw, known one-off costs, and expected tax set-asides if they know them.
- Show **every formula** and intermediate result; if a code tool is available, compute in a script or spreadsheet the founder can reuse, rather than mental math.
- Separate **committed** from **hoped-for** revenue; base case uses committed only.
- **Not tax/legal/investment advice** — recommend an accountant for taxes, entity, and payroll questions.

## Standard outputs
1. **Runway snapshot:** cash, gross burn, net burn, runway months, and the date cash hits zero / hits a safety floor (default floor: 3 months of fixed costs — adjustable).
2. **Scenarios:** base (committed only), downside (revenue −X%, costs +Y%), upside — X/Y chosen with the founder.
3. **Break-even:** customers or revenue needed at current pricing and margins.
4. **Decision impact:** "If I hire a contractor at $Z/month starting in month N, runway changes from A to B months."
5. **12-month month-by-month table** exported as CSV.

## Warning lights to flag
Runway < 6 months without a funded plan; revenue concentrated in one customer; growing burn without growing revenue; founder not paying themselves enough to be sustainable.
