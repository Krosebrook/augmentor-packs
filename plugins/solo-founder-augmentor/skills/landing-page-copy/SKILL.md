# Landing Page Copy

## Inputs
Positioning (from `offer-positioning`), best-fit customer, the single conversion action, customer quotes from interviews/reviews/support, proof the founder actually has (testimonials with permission, numbers, logos, case studies).

## Hard rules
- **No fabricated social proof.** Never write fake testimonials, user counts, ratings, logos, or "as seen in." Use `[TESTIMONIAL NEEDED]` placeholders.
- **No unverifiable claims** ("#1", "fastest", "guaranteed results") unless the founder has evidence; regulated areas (health, finance, earnings) need extra care.
- **Steal the customer's words** — interview language beats copywriter language.

## Page skeleton (default; adapt)
1. **Hero:** headline = the outcome for the specific customer (not a clever slogan); subhead = how/for whom/what makes it different; primary CTA; supporting visual description.
2. **Problem:** the trigger moment in the customer's words.
3. **How it works:** 3 steps.
4. **Benefits:** outcome-led bullets, each backed by a feature ("So you can… because…").
5. **Proof:** real testimonials/case studies/numbers (or placeholders).
6. **Objection handling / FAQ:** price, switching cost, trust, "is this for me?"
7. **Offer & CTA:** what they get, price or next step, risk reversal.
8. **Final CTA.**

## Quality checks
- **5-second test:** from the hero alone, can a stranger say what it is, who it's for, and what to do next?
- One primary CTA, repeated; no competing asks.
- Cut adjectives that any competitor could also claim ("powerful", "seamless").
- Reading level: plain and concrete.

## Output
Full page copy by section + **3 headline variants** with the angle each takes (outcome / pain / differentiation) for A/B testing, plus a list of placeholders the founder must fill with real proof.
