# Template: Planning (trip, event, move)

Ask one question at a time.

## Question bank
- What does a great outcome look like? What would make it a disaster?
- Dates: fixed or flexible? Any hard deadline?
- Who's involved? Any needs (kids, mobility, diet, pets, accessibility)?
- Total budget, and what it must cover (travel, lodging, food, deposits, movers, buffer)?
- What do you want to do yourself vs pay someone for?
- **Trip:** where from, rough destination ideas, pace (relaxed or packed), passport or visa status in general terms, travel insurance stance.
- **Event:** number of guests, indoor or outdoor, must-have elements, vendors already booked.
- **Move:** from and to (city level only), home size, timeline, what's being sold or stored, lease or closing dates.

## Push-backs that help
- "If the budget had to shrink by 20%, what goes first?"
- "What happens if it rains, the flight is cancelled, or the movers are late? What's plan B?"
- "Is that date fixed by something real, or just preferred?"

## Criteria to carry into research
Cost vs budget (with buffer), timing fit, cancellation and refund policies, current entry, permit, or booking rules (these change; always check official sources), reviews from recent dates.
