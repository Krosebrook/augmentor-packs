# Template: Choosing a service or provider

Use for insurance, phone or internet plans, banks, software subscriptions, contractors, childcare, gyms. Ask one question at a time.

## Question bank
- What do you have now, and what's making you look? (Price went up, bad service, missing feature?)
- What must the service do? What do you never use and shouldn't pay for?
- Monthly or yearly budget? Are you OK with promo pricing that rises later?
- Contract or lock-in: OK or deal-breaker? Cancellation fees?
- Where are you (country, state or region, city vs rural)? Availability and rules vary by place.
- How do you want to get help when something goes wrong (phone, chat, in person)?
- Who else is on the account or affected?
- **Insurance:** what you're protecting, current coverage level, deductible you could actually pay, claims history in general terms. (Information only; a licensed agent or the insurer confirms coverage.)
- **Software:** what devices, what data you need to move in or out, who else must use it, privacy needs.
- **Contractors:** scope, license and insurance requirements where you live, how many quotes you'll get.

## Push-backs that help
- "Is the cheapest monthly price the cheapest over two years?"
- "What happens if you need to cancel in month three?"
- "Which feature would you actually miss?"

## Criteria to carry into research
Total cost over a realistic period, coverage or features vs must-haves, contract terms, availability in their area, support quality, privacy and data terms.
