# Template: Buying a thing

Use for tech (laptops, phones, TVs), appliances, cars, furniture, tools. Ask one question at a time; skip what's already answered.

## Question bank
- What will you use it for, most days? What's the one task it *must* do well?
- What do you have now? What's wrong with it? Could it be repaired instead? (Check: is buying the right move at all?)
- Budget: a number or range? Does that include extras (cases, installation, delivery, insurance, fuel, subscriptions)?
- New, refurbished, or used: which are you open to?
- Size, weight, space, or compatibility limits? (Measure the space; list devices it must work with.)
- Brands you love or refuse, and why? (Probe: real experience or reputation?)
- How long do you expect to keep it?
- Deal-breakers: noise, subscriptions, repairability, warranty length, where it's sold?
- When do you need it? Can you wait for a sale?
- **Cars only:** miles per year, city or highway, passengers and cargo, charging or parking at home, total cost of ownership (insurance, fuel or charging, maintenance) vs sticker price.

## Push-backs that help
- "Do you need the newest model, or would last year's do the job for less?"
- "Which spec would you actually notice day to day?"
- "What happens if it breaks in year two? Who fixes it?"

## Criteria to carry into research
Price (total, not just sticker), fit for the main task, reliability and repair record, warranty and return policy, ongoing costs, availability where they live.
