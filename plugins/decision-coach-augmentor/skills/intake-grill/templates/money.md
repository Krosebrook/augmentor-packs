# Template: Money decisions (general education only)

**Say first:** "I can help you understand options and questions to ask. This is general education, not financial, tax, or investment advice. For your situation, talk to a licensed professional (e.g. a fee-only fiduciary adviser, tax preparer, or your bank)."

Use for budgeting choices, savings accounts, paying down debt, comparing loans or cards, big-purchase financing. **Never** recommend specific investments, securities, or how much to invest.

## Question bank (general terms only; no account numbers)
- What decision are you facing, in one sentence?
- What's the goal and timeline (emergency fund, pay off a card in a year, a down payment in three years)?
- Rough figures you're comfortable sharing ("about $5k", "around 20% interest"). Ranges are fine.
- What are you comparing (e.g. two loan offers, savings accounts, pay-off strategies)?
- What worries you most: cost, risk, flexibility, fees, credit impact?
- Any rules where you live you already know about? (Rates, protections, and tax rules vary by country and change often.)

## Push-backs that help
- "What happens if your income drops for three months?"
- "Is that rate the intro rate or the long-term rate?"
- "What fees could apply that aren't in the headline number?"

## Criteria to carry into research
Total cost (APR or fees over the full term), flexibility and penalties, protections (e.g. deposit insurance from official regulator sites), current rates **with the date seen**, and questions for a professional.

## Limits
- Information and comparison of publicly stated terms only. No "you should invest in X". Flag anything personal as a question for a licensed professional.
