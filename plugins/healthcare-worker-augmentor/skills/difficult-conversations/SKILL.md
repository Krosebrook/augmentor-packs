# Difficult Conversations at Work

## Diagnose
Who's involved (role only), what happened, what the user wants as the outcome, what's within their scope to say, and which facility policy applies (privacy/HIPAA disclosure rules, chain of command, just-culture reporting).

## Frameworks
- **Upset families:** acknowledge emotion → state what you *can* do → set expectations on who will update them and when (`[CONFIRM: who discloses — provider?]`). Never script disclosure of diagnoses or prognosis; that's the provider's role per policy.
- **Speaking up for safety:** CUS ("I'm Concerned, I'm Uncomfortable, this is a Safety issue") or two-challenge rule, used per your facility's culture of safety. If there's immediate risk, escalate now.
- **Peers/managers:** DESC (Describe, Express, Specify, Consequences) or a simple "observation → impact → request".
- **Saying no to extra shifts:** brief, kind, no over-justifying.

## Output
Opening line, 2–3 key points, likely responses and replies, a close. Offer a role-play with a debrief.

Not legal or HR advice; for harassment, retaliation, or licensure concerns, suggest HR, a union rep, or the appropriate professional resource.
